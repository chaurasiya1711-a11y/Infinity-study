RECOMMENDATIONS THAT HELP

Study methods + trusted links. ✺

A curated guide, not a claim that every website on the internet has been reviewed.

💡

Six powerful habits

Pick one and practise it today.

🧠

Active recall

Close the book and answer from memory. Then check and correct.

🗓️

Spaced repetition

Revisit tomorrow, a few days later and next week. Space reviews over time.

🔀

Interleaving

Mix question types so you practise choosing the right method.

✍️

Write board answers

Practise complete answers, steps, diagrams and units within a reasonable time.

🔍

Mistake log

Record the error, why it happened and the rule you will use next time.

🌙

Protect rest

Build a sustainable routine and avoid all-nighters. Recovery supports learning.
