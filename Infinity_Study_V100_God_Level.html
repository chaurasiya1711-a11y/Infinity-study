A BETTER WAY TO STUDY

Recommendations ✺

Study techniques, trusted learning resources and practical tips for your Class 10 journey.

💡

Coach's playbook

Pick one strategy and try it today.

🧠

Active recall

Close your notes and answer questions from memory. Then check and correct.

Use after every topic
🗓️

Spaced revision

Revisit a topic tomorrow, then in a few days, then next week. Short repeats beat one giant cram.

Build long-term memory
🔀

Mix your practice

After learning a method, mix it with questions from earlier chapters so you learn when to use it.

Especially for Maths
🗣️

Teach it simply

Explain a concept as if teaching a younger student. Gaps in your explanation show what to revise.

Science & SST
✍️

Write board answers

Practise writing complete answers, diagrams, steps and units under a reasonable time limit.

Exam readiness
🌙

Protect your sleep

A repeatable schedule and enough sleep help attention and memory. Don't build a plan around all-nighters.
